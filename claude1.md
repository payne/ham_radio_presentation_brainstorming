# Monthly Educational Programs for Amateur Radio Club

A year-long schedule of educational programs with three alternatives for each month.

## January - Winter Operations & Emergency Communications

1. **Cold Weather Field Operations** - Best practices for operating in winter conditions, battery performance, and weatherproofing equipment
   - [ARRL Emergency Communications Training](https://www.arrl.org/emergency-communications-training)

2. **ARES/RACES Introduction** - Getting involved with emergency communications in your community
   - [ARRL ARES](https://www.arrl.org/ares)

3. **SKYWARN Training** - National Weather Service severe weather spotter certification
   - [SKYWARN](https://www.weather.gov/skywarn/)

## February - Digital Modes Deep Dive

1. **FT8 Operations Workshop** - Setting up WSJT-X, operating procedures, and maximizing contacts
   - [WSJT-X](https://physics.princeton.edu/pulsar/k1jt/wsjtx.html)

2. **VARA Modes (FM/HF)** - Exploring VARA for Winlink and VarAC chat applications
   - [Winlink VARA](https://www.winlink.org/tags/vara)

3. **JS8Call Messaging** - Weak signal keyboard-to-keyboard communications
   - [JS8Call](http://js8call.com/)

## March - Antenna Theory & Building

1. **Antenna Fundamentals** - Understanding SWR, resonance, and radiation patterns
   - [ARRL Antenna Book](https://www.arrl.org/shop/ARRL-Antenna-Book-Softcover-25th-Edition/)

2. **Build Night: J-Pole for 2M/70cm** - Hands-on construction of a simple, effective VHF/UHF antenna

3. **NVIS Antennas for EmComm** - Near Vertical Incidence Skywave for regional communications
   - [ARRL NVIS](https://www.arrl.org/nvis)

## April - Contesting & DXing

1. **Contest Operating Basics** - Introduction to amateur radio contests and operating techniques
   - [Contest Calendar](https://www.contestcalendar.com/)

2. **DX Spotting & Cluster Networks** - Using tools to find and work rare DX
   - [DX Summit](https://www.dxsummit.fi/)

3. **Logging Software Comparison** - Demo of N3FJP, Log4OM, and other popular logging programs

## May - VHF/UHF & Satellites

1. **FM Satellite Operations** - Working SO-50, AO-91, and other FM satellites with basic equipment
   - [AMSAT FM Satellites](https://www.amsat.org/fm-satellites/)

2. **APRS Deep Dive** - Automatic Packet Reporting System setup and applications
   - [APRS.org](https://www.aprs.org/)

3. **6 Meter Magic** - Operating on the "Magic Band" during Es season
   - [ARRL 6 Meters](https://www.arrl.org/6-meters)

## June - Field Day Preparation

1. **Field Day Planning Workshop** - Station setup, operations, and bonus points
   - [ARRL Field Day](https://www.arrl.org/field-day)

2. **Portable Power Solutions** - Solar panels, batteries, and generators for field operations

3. **Quick Deploy Antennas** - Setting up wire antennas efficiently in the field

## July - RF Safety & Station Grounding

1. **RF Safety & Exposure Limits** - Understanding FCC regulations and protecting yourself
   - [ARRL RF Exposure](https://www.arrl.org/rf-exposure)

2. **Station Grounding Best Practices** - Proper grounding for safety and RFI reduction
   - [ARRL Grounding](https://www.arrl.org/grounding)

3. **RFI/EMI Troubleshooting** - Identifying and eliminating interference issues

## August - QRP & Portable Operations

1. **QRP Operating Techniques** - Making contacts with 5 watts or less
   - [QRP ARCI](https://www.qrparci.org/)

2. **SOTA (Summits on the Air)** - Mountain hiking and portable HF operations
   - [SOTA](https://www.sota.org.uk/)

3. **POTA (Parks on the Air)** - Activating and hunting parks for awards
   - [Parks on the Air](https://parksontheair.com/)

## September - DMR & Digital Voice

1. **DMR Basics & Codeplug Programming** - Getting started with Digital Mobile Radio
   - [RadioReference DMR Database](https://www.radioreference.com/apps/db/?aid=7731)

2. **D-STAR Operations** - Understanding Icom's digital voice system
   - [D-STAR Info](https://www.dstarinfo.com/)

3. **System Fusion (C4FM)** - Yaesu's digital mode and Wires-X networking
   - [System Fusion](https://systemfusion.yaesu.com/)

## October - HF Propagation & Solar Cycle

1. **Understanding Propagation** - Ionospheric layers, sunspots, and band conditions
   - [SWS Educational Resources](https://www.sws.bom.gov.au/Educational/2/3/5)

2. **Using VOACAP & Propagation Tools** - Predicting band openings and best frequencies
   - [VOACAP](https://www.voacap.com/)

3. **Gray Line & Scatter Propagation** - Advanced propagation modes for DXing

## November - Homebrewing & Kit Building

1. **Kit Building Workshop** - Assemble a simple QRP transmitter or antenna tuner
   - [QRP Kits](https://www.qrpkits.com/)

2. **Introduction to Arduino for Ham Radio** - Microcontrollers for station automation
   - [Arduino](https://www.arduino.cc/)

3. **Raspberry Pi Ham Radio Applications** - Running digital modes, logging, and remote operation
   - [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.org/)

## December - Year in Review & Social

1. **Member Presentations** - Club members share their best ham radio experiences from the year

2. **Show & Tell: New Gear** - Members demonstrate new equipment and projects

3. **Planning for Next Year** - Club activities, upgrades, and Field Day site selection
